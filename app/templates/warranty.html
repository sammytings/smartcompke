What Your Warranty Covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware faults that occur during normal use of the product. If a genuine fault is confirmed, the product may be repaired, replaced or serviced by the manufacturer or an authorised service centre.

Warranty Does Not Cover

Certain situations are outside the manufacturer's warranty.

Liquid Damage

Damage caused by water, coffee, drinks or any other liquid is not covered by the manufacturer warranty.

Physical Damage

Broken screens, cracked casings, accidental drops, impact damage and misuse are not covered.

Power Surges

Damage caused by electrical surges, lightning or unstable power supply is excluded from warranty.

Unauthorized Repairs

Opening or repairing the product by an unauthorized technician immediately voids the manufacturer's warranty.

How To Make A Warranty Claim

Our warranty claim process is designed to be simple and transparent.

Step 1

Present your purchase receipt or invoice together with the product.

Step 2

Our technicians inspect the product and confirm whether the issue qualifies for warranty.

Step 3

The product is submitted to the authorised service centre for repair or replacement where applicable.

Step 4

Once servicing is complete, you will be notified to collect your product or arrange delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim warranty without a receipt?
A purchase receipt or invoice is normally required to verify the purchase date and warranty period.
How long does a warranty repair take?
Repair timelines depend on the manufacturer and availability of spare parts. Most repairs are completed as quickly as possible.
Will I receive a replacement product immediately?
Replacement depends on the manufacturer's warranty policy and product availability.
Does upgrading RAM or SSD void warranty?
Some manufacturers allow upgrades while others require authorised technicians. Contact us before upgrading your device.

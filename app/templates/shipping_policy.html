1 Order Processing

Once your order has been placed, our team will verify product availability, payment status and delivery information before preparing your order for dispatch.

Orders placed during normal business hours are usually processed within the same business day. Orders received on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

If we require additional information before processing your order, one of our representatives will contact you using the phone number or email address provided during checkout.

2 Delivery Areas

SmartComputersKE delivers to customers across Kenya through our own delivery team and trusted courier partners.

  • Same-day delivery may be available within selected areas of Nairobi.
  • Next-day delivery is available for most major towns.
  • Remote locations may require additional delivery time.
  • Some oversized products may require special transport arrangements.

3 Shipping Charges

Shipping fees are calculated based on your delivery location, the weight of your order and the selected shipping method.

Delivery charges displayed during checkout are estimates and may vary for large, fragile or bulky products.

Important: Customers will always be informed of any additional delivery charges before an order is dispatched.

4 Estimated Delivery Timelines

  • Nairobi: Same day or within 24 hours.
  • Major towns: 1–3 business days.
  • Remote counties: 2–5 business days.
  • Special orders: Delivery timelines will be communicated individually.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may change depending on weather conditions, courier schedules or unforeseen circumstances.

5 Order Tracking

After your order has been dispatched, you may receive updates through SMS, email or phone call depending on the delivery method selected.

Customers may also contact our support team at any time for the latest delivery status.

6 Delivery Delays

Although we strive to deliver every order on time, delays may occasionally occur due to factors beyond our control.

  • Heavy rainfall or extreme weather.
  • Road closures or traffic disruptions.
  • Public holidays.
  • Courier operational delays.
  • Incomplete delivery information.

Where possible, customers will be informed promptly if a delay occurs.

7 Receiving Your Order

Please inspect your package immediately upon delivery.

  • Confirm the package is sealed.
  • Verify that the correct products have been delivered.
  • Report any visible damage immediately.
  • Notify us within 24 hours if an item is missing or incorrect.

8 International Shipping

At this time, SmartComputersKE primarily serves customers within Kenya.

International shipping may be arranged for selected products upon request. Additional shipping charges, customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise stated.

Returns & Refund Policy

Returns & Refunds

At Smartcomputerske Technologies, customer satisfaction is our priority. We supply genuine laptops, desktops, printers, accessories and networking equipment across Kenya — if you receive the wrong item, a damaged product, or something with a manufacturer defect, we'll work quickly to put it right.

Know before you buy

Our Return Policy

The most common situations we handle, and how each one is resolved.

Wrong Item Delivered

If we accidentally deliver the wrong product, we'll replace it at no additional cost.

Damaged During Delivery

Report transport damage immediately so we can arrange a replacement or inspection.

Manufacturer Defects

Faults caused by a manufacturing defect are handled through our warranty procedure.

Proof of Purchase

A valid invoice or receipt is required before any return, replacement or refund.

Eligible for Return

  • Product received damaged during delivery
  • Wrong product supplied
  • Manufacturer defect found on first use
  • Missing accessories from the package
  • Product significantly different from your order

Not Eligible for Return

  • Products physically damaged after delivery
  • Software licenses and activation keys
  • Products missing original packaging
  • Products modified by unauthorized technicians
  • Opened consumables (e.g. printer ink or toner)

Eligibility at a Glance

Situation Eligible? Notes
Wrong item supplied Yes Replacement provided.
Factory defect Yes Warranty process applies.
Damaged in shipping Yes Report immediately after delivery.
Change of mind Depends Item must be unopened and unused.
Used products No Cannot be returned unless defective.

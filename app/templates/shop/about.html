Who we are

Making quality technology accessible to every Kenyan

Founded to become one of Kenya's most trusted computer and electronics suppliers.

Smartcomputerske Technologies was founded with a straightforward goal: become one of Kenya's most trusted computer and electronics suppliers. Technology powers businesses, classrooms and homes, so we're selective about what reaches our shelves — every product is sourced for quality, reliability and value.

Our team spends as much time listening as selling. Before we recommend a laptop, a router or a full office fit-out, we ask what you actually need it to do — then match that to your budget, not the other way around.

  • Authorised sourcing

    Bought through trusted manufacturers and authorised distributors — not grey-market imports.

  • Advice before the sale

    Recommendations based on real use cases, not the highest-margin item on the shelf.

  • Support after the sale

    Repairs, upgrades and technical consultation continue long after checkout.
